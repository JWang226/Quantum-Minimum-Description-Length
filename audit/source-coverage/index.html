    Conventions¶

    • All logarithms base two; memory is log dimension rather than rounded physical-qubit count. (article.tex:77–83)
    • d and spectrum fixed as n grows; constants are not uniform near collisions or vanishing positive eigenvalues. (article.tex:99–99)
